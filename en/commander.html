Commander guide: choose a plan and start building

Find a commander you want to play, choose how you want the deck to work, and build around that plan.

Start with the basics

A typical Commander deck contains 100 cards: your commander and 99 other cards. Build within your commander's color identity, and generally use only one copy of a card other than basic lands. Start by choosing a commander whose abilities support the experience you want.

Choose how you want to play

Each link below opens the builder with that strategy selected. The builder currently uses Japanese interface labels.

Balanced (Japanese UI)Interaction (Japanese UI)Speed (Japanese UI)Combos (Japanese UI)
  • Balanced: support the commander's theme with ramp, card draw and interaction.
  • Interaction: focus on removal, counters and protecting your board.
  • Speed: use ramp and cost reduction to develop your plan sooner.
  • Combos: build around a clear combination and the cards that find or protect it.

Talk about the game you want

Before playing, discuss your deck's plan, combo speed and the power level your group expects. Use that conversation to decide whether your deck fits the table.

Check the deck's supporting roles

Look beyond the cards that directly match your theme. Review your mana base, ramp, card draw, removal, protection and graveyard interaction. Some cards cover more than one role, so consider how they work together rather than adding separate quotas blindly.
