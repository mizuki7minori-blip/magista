ORIGINAL DUNGEON EXPLORER

Tower of Mist

You take a turn, then enemies take theirs. Explore a different dungeon each time, collect swords, shields, wands and projectiles, and challenge the colossus on floor 10. Plan your next move at your own pace.

Turn-based roguelikeDesktop & mobileEnglish & JapaneseNormal / Nightmare
Play Tower of Mist (new tab)How to play

The game opens on its own page. A ChatGPT sign-in is required to play. Adventures autosave for each account, and your existing save remains available.

Your goal: conquer floor 10

  1. Open the game and sign in with ChatGPT.
  2. Choose New Adventure to select a class and difficulty, or continue your saved adventure.
  3. Collect items and find the stairs to the next floor.
  4. Defeat the floor 10 colossus, then descend the stairs to win.

Using items and skills also takes a turn. Watch your HP and satiety. Death resets that adventure's level and inventory.

Four classes, new skills as you level

Warrior
Strong melee attacks. Learn Whirlwind and Royal Slash.
Rogue
Specialize in projectiles and movement, with Smoke Screen and Venom Storm.
Mage
Use wands and magic: Frost Bolt, Flame Wave and Thunder Lance.
Guardian
High HP and defense, with healing and status recovery.

Learn new skills at levels 3 and 5. For a tougher challenge, Nightmare adds enemies and reduces supplies.

Desktop controls

Arrows / WASDMove or attack an adjacent enemy
JAttack / open an adjacent chest
K / SpaceWait
IOpen / close inventory
LChoose a skill
Enter / EscDescend stairs / close a dialog

Mobile controls & language

Move with the directional pad. A attacks or opens chests, B waits, X opens items, and Y descends stairs. Use the Skill button to choose an ability.

Switch languages with the game's English / Japanese button. Choose a direction in the inventory to use wands and projectiles.
